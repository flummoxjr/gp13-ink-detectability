# PREREG — Bet A: input-noise-matched training for 9 µm ink transfer (DRAFT v0, 2026-09-01)

**Status: DRAFT.** Becomes a pre-registration when (1) the corrected 500p2a anchor from
`bench/p2a_v3` is filled into §5 and (2) this file is committed to git *before* any training
run starts. Nothing in §5 may change after that commit except by a dated addendum.

Companion: SEPTEMBER_PLAN.md §2.1; the benchmark: `bench/BENCHMARK.md`.

## 1. Claim under test

`ink_9um` fails to transfer to eligible scrolls because its training inputs (24 of 29
representations are 2.399 µm volumes pooled ×64 to ~9.6 µm) are systematically cleaner than any
native ~9 µm acquisition; the model learns clean-texture priors a native volume cannot satisfy.
**If true**, degrading the pooled training inputs to the *measured* noise statistics of the eligible
scrolls raises held-out native transfer. **If false**, the transfer gap is not input noise and the
community should stop spending on augmentation and move to labels (Bets B/C/E).

Prior art that does *not* test this: villa #1582's domain-match arm re-weighted native exposure
without degrading pooled inputs; histogram matching (Jinhojeong; our f05 doubling) is an intensity
transform, not a noise/bandwidth transform.

## 1b. Prior weakened before launch (2026-09-02) — read before spending

The corrected 500p2a measurement bears directly on the claim in §1. 500p2a is a **2.215 µm scan pooled to
9.36 µm** — the same representation family as 24 of ink_9um's 29 training inputs (2.399 µm pooled to 9.6 µm),
i.e. the *clean* input class the hypothesis says the model has learned priors for. It still reads at chance
(0.52), with the harness certified on the in-domain control (0.9991). If the transfer gap were mainly
input-noise mismatch, a pooled-class foreign input should transfer *better* than native — it does not.
Bet A's honest prior therefore drops (from ~25–35% to ~10–15%); the failure looks scroll-specific (texture /
ink prior), which is Bet C's premise (corpus size, not acquisition domain). Recommendation for the weekly gate
review: run arm 0 (the LOSO anchor, needed by every bet) and decide on arms 1/2 only after Bet C's first
number, unless the local 4090 is idle. Recorded here so the decision is visible, not silent.

## 2. Design

Leave-one-scroll-out on the published ink_9um recipe (`configs/aligned21_hybrid_3d2d.json` +
`aligned21_fixed_scroll_prior.json`), configs generated by `bench/vendor/make_holdout_config.py
--exclude-scroll 0139` (khj1222, PR #1608; renormalised quotas {1667: 40, Paris4: 20, 0814: 4}).
Held out: **all of PHerc0139** (the only scroll with native 9.362 µm human labels), so its five
native segments (w035/w039/w040/w041/w044) are honest held-out ground truth.

| arm | training input | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | LOSO baseline, recipe unchanged | reproduce khj1222's published number first (anchor gate, §4) |
| 1 | pooled inputs degraded per batch to the target class: Gaussian blur to the measured PSD cliff (σ so the 0.25–0.35 cyc/px roll-off matches native), additive Gaussian noise to the measured structural SNR at q = 0.25 cyc/px, uint8 window + clipping matched to the release window; parameters drawn per batch from the 14-scroll distribution in `out/k2b_index/` (`snr_q025`, `bandwidth_cyc_px`, `dn_headroom`) | the hypothesis |
| 2 | PSD-whitened inputs at train *and* test time (each volume canonicalised by its own measured noise spectrum) | the cheap opposite strategy; distinct from histogram matching |

2 seeds per arm (42, 43), 40–75 k steps on the recipe's schedule, identical everything else.
The native-5 representations of 0139 are *removed from training* in every arm (they are the test).

## 3. Evaluation (all on `bench/BENCHMARK.md`)

- Primary: pixel AUC on the five held-out native 0139 segments (labels: HF
  `ink_9um/labels/native9-scrollprizeorg-21slices`), both directions, best direction reported with the other.
- Secondary: 500p2a win1/win2/win3 at the corrected 2.215 µm pitch (iso depth mode); Frag1.
- Every run reports the four positive controls of §3 of the benchmark from the same harness.
- Seed spread of arm 0 is the noise floor; a difference smaller than it is "no effect".

## 4. Anchor gate (before any arm 1/2 spend)

Arm 0 must land within 0.05 AUC-equivalent of khj1222's published LOSO number on 0139
(+0.17 F1 over the everything-is-ink floor; convert with the same threshold sweep). If it does not,
STOP: our reproduction of the recipe is wrong and no arm comparison means anything.

## 5. Decision rule (to be frozen)

- **PASS** iff, on held-out native 0139, arm 1 *or* arm 2 beats arm 0 by ≥ **+0.05 mean AUC** over the
  five segments (both seeds; the improvement must exceed 2× the arm-0 seed spread), **and** on 500p2a
  win1 (iso) the arm reaches ≥ **max(0.65, A₃ + 0.05)** where A₃ = the corrected anchor from
  `bench/p2a_v3` (`results/expA_baseline.json → corrected_anchor_auc`). A₃ = **0.5211** (win1 iso forward, `experiments/p2a_v3`, 2026-09-02; fit17 0.5301; win2/win3 in the same results file). Hence the second clause is **500p2a win1 (iso) ≥ 0.65**.
- **KILLED** otherwise. The augmentation code, configs and held-out numbers ship either way.
- If A₃ ≥ 0.85 the second clause is replaced by "does not *reduce* 500p2a below A₃ − 0.03" (the anchor
  is then already at curve-anchoring quality and the bet is about the native tier only).

## 6. Cost and infrastructure

- 6 runs × 6–12 GPU-h on a 5090 pod ($0.69/h) ≈ $25–50; or the local 4090 overnight for arms at $0
  (warn Ben: no League on training nights).
- Training data: the ink_9um dataset (HF `ink_9um/labels/…` + surface volumes on S3, 29 representations)
  is pulled on the pod; never through the laptop.
- Pod discipline: gist-launched script, status server first, prereg locked in `out/prereg.json`,
  `scripts/pod_guard.py` with a deadline; the training recipe's own checkpoints every 10 k steps.
- Namespace: `betA_*`.

## 7. What each outcome means for the plan

- PASS → the September instrument exists; screen the Tier-1 patches with it (Lane B), Bet E prep.
- KILLED with arm 0 healthy → input noise is not the gap; October's Bet C (max-corpus native generalist)
  becomes the only model bet; publish the null with the harness.
- Arm 0 fails the anchor gate → the recipe reproduction is the September deliverable (a bug report
  or a config fix upstream), and Bet A slips a week.
